# Enterprise AI Customer Support & Sales Multi-Agent Platform

An enterprise-grade AI automation platform built with **n8n** that automates customer support, sales operations, CRM management, meeting scheduling, and ticket handling using multiple specialized AI Agents.

---

# Overview

This workflow simulates a real SaaS company's customer support and sales department.

Instead of relying on a single AI assistant, the platform consists of multiple intelligent agents that collaborate to process customer requests, answer questions, generate quotations, create support tickets, update the CRM, schedule meetings, and notify both customers and employees automatically.

The system is designed to reduce manual work while providing fast, accurate, and scalable customer service.
